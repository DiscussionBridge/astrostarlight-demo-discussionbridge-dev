## Simple mode

This page uses `discussionCommentsDisplay: "simple"`.

Astro reads a public Discourse topic and renders its replies in a bounded,
site-native comments frame. The source site needs no DiscussionBridge
connection credential, and the forum does not need the DiscussionBridge
receiver plugin for this mode.

Simple mode deliberately keeps the browser surface small. Readers follow the
topic link when they want to reply, sign in, manage notifications, or use the
full Discourse interface.
